# Phase 0: intake

Ask what hurts today and what success looks like. Use the intake template. Do not assume a file cleanup is the whole goal.

Record tools and versions, OS, launch method, profiles, owner-confirmed accounts, scopes, repositories, cloud destinations, and managed restrictions. Record identity as an owner-provided label, not a credential export.

Ask about workflows, valued plugins, histories, memories, accessibility, UI preferences, recurring jobs, cost limits, and policies that must be enforced. A low-overhead command-line solution may miss a required GUI.

Agree on scan roots and excluded data. Ask the owner to identify an opaque vault and mixed credential-bearing files by name only. Do not enumerate environment values. Have the owner run sign-in in a separate uncaptured terminal if needed.

Choose a private run folder outside scanned roots. Keep its sharing and retention rules explicit. Estimate room for canonical copies, backups, retained originals, and interrupted output.

Record who may decide routine choices and who approves account, system, or outward-facing changes. Do not infer another account is available as a fallback.

Gate: the owner confirms scope, read boundaries, desired outcomes, and approval responsibilities. No restructuring starts here.
